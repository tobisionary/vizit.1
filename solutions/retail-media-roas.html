FAQ

Retail media questions, answered.

What performance marketing and retail media leaders ask us most about ROAS, CTR, and CPC on Amazon, Walmart, Target, and Kroger.

+Why is my retail media ROAS low?
Most retail media underperformance is caused by the destination PDP, not the bid or the targeting. The hero image determines whether shoppers tap. The carousel determines whether they convert. If either fails, every click underperforms regardless of how precisely the audience is targeted. You can't bid your way out of a failing PDP. You need to score and then fix the core assets first.
+How do I lower my Amazon ACoS?
ACoS is a function of conversion rate and CPC, and both are driven by visual content more than most teams realize. A weak hero image lowers CTR, which raises CPC. A weak PDP lowers conversion, which raises ACoS even further. The fastest path to lower ACoS is fixing the visual content shoppers see. Brands that bring their PDPs to Vizit Product Page Score of 80 or above typically see ACoS drop within weeks or days of making the update.
+How do I lower my Amazon CPC?
Amazon CPC is influenced by relevance score, which is influenced by CTR, which is driven primarily by hero image performance. Raising a hero image's Vizit Score improves CTR, which improves relevance, which lowers effective CPC. A strategy to lower your retail media CPC should start with image measurement and image optimization.
+How do I improve Sponsored Products CTR?
Sponsored Products CTR is determined almost entirely by the hero image, because that's the only visual element shoppers see in the search grid before deciding whether to tap. Most underperforming Sponsored Products listings have hero images that fail to clear the visual threshold needed to win attention against competitors. Vizit scores hero images on the specific dimensions that drive category-leading CTRs on a scale from 0–100.
+Does image quality affect Amazon sponsored ads?
Yes. Every sponsored placement on Amazon is won or lost at the hero image. Image quality affects CTR, which affects relevance, which affects CPC and placement. A low Vizit Score on the hero image directly suppresses campaign performance, even with perfect targeting and bidding.
