FAQ

Hero image questions, answered.

The questions ecommerce, retail media, and creative leaders ask us most about the asset that decides the click.

+What is an Amazon hero image?
An Amazon hero image (also called the main product image or main image) is the primary photograph displayed for a product across Amazon search results, sponsored placements, and product detail pages. It is the single most important visual asset a brand owns on Amazon: it determines whether a shopper clicks the product, and it appears identically across mobile, desktop, and AI-driven discovery surfaces. Amazon requires the hero image to have a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), no text or graphic overlays, and a minimum resolution of 1000×1000 pixels for zoom functionality.
+What makes a good Amazon hero image?
A high-performing Amazon hero image does four things at once: it communicates the brand, product, and variant instantly at thumbnail scale on mobile; it speaks the visual language shoppers in its category respond to; it stands out distinctly against the competing products surrounding it on the search grid; and it meets Amazon's technical requirements for resolution, background, and composition. Vizit's Hero Image Score measures all four — Relevance, Distinctiveness, Aesthetic Impact, and Retail Readiness — on a 0–100 scale, calibrated to the live competitive context of each category.
+How do I increase glance views on Amazon?
Glance views — Amazon's term for unique product page visits — are driven by what shoppers see in search results, not by the product page itself. The hero image is the asset that determines whether a shopper clicks through to generate a glance view: title, price, and reviews are only consulted after the click. Optimizing the hero image for thumbnail clarity, mobile readability, and the visual triggers proven to convert in your category is the highest-leverage way to increase glance views, lift Amazon CTR, and improve sponsored ad ROAS — because every paid placement uses the same hero image as organic search.
+What is thumb-stopping content?
Thumb-stopping content is visual content engineered to interrupt a shopper's scroll on mobile: to command attention in the fraction of a second before the thumb keeps moving. On mobile-first commerce surfaces (Amazon, Walmart, Instagram, TikTok Shop, etc.), thumb-stopping power is the precondition for every downstream metric: click, consideration, and conversion. Vizit's Hero Image Score measures thumb-stopping performance with shoppers in each specific category, determining whether your image actually stops the scroll.
