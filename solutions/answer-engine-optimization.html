04 — One image, two customers

Engineer your hero images for the algorithm's eyes.

Vizit optimizes your product images for both audiences simultaneously: the human shopper making the split-second judgment, and the AI system deciding what gets surfaced. This is Visual AEO — where the next generation of digital shelf wins are happening.

Product clarity

AI systems recommend what they can identify with confidence. Cluttered backgrounds, poor contrast, and bad framing reduce that confidence — and your recommendation likelihood with it.

Contextual relevance

Multimodal AI matches products to shopper intent by reading visual context. An image without setting, usage, or lifestyle cues gives the AI less to match against a query — and more reason to recommend a competitor.

Visual hierarchy

Color, texture, scale, and distinguishing features must be legible at thumbnail size. If the AI can't resolve what makes your product distinctive, it defaults to the brand whose image communicates it clearly.

Readability & accessibility

Text overlays that fail contrast thresholds don't just lose the human shopper. They reduce image legibility for machine vision systems evaluating the asset — and that ambiguity works against you in AI-powered discovery.

FAQ

Visual AEO questions, answered.

What ecommerce and AI-readiness leaders ask us most about getting recommended by Rufus, Sparky, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.

+What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) for ecommerce?
AEO is the practice of optimizing product content to be surfaced, recommended, and cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Walmart's Sparky, and Amazon Rufus. For ecommerce, this includes optimizing product images for multimodal AI systems that judge product recommendations based on visual content, not just text. The visual layer of AEO is the most under-invested side of the discipline — and the one that has a big impact on which products get recommended.
+How do AI shopping agents choose which products to recommend?
Modern AI shopping agents are multimodal, which means they evaluate both text and images. Visual signals are one of several inputs, but they're the input that determines whether a product clears the recommendation threshold in the first place. The AI evaluates images for things like product clarity, contextual relevance to the prompt, visual hierarchy, and readability. Products whose images fail these checks are deprioritized, regardless of how well the listing copy is written or how strong the reviews are.
+How do I optimize product images for ChatGPT, Rufus, and Perplexity?
Optimize for machine readability (clean object boundaries, high contrast, uncluttered framing), contextual signals (lifestyle and usage cues that match likely prompts), and visual hierarchy (distinguishing features legible at thumbnail scale). This is what Visual AEO measures and what Vizit scores and optimizes simultaneously for human shoppers and AI systems.
+What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
GEO is the practice of optimizing content to be cited and recommended by generative AI systems — essentially SEO for the AI era. For product pages, GEO covers both structured data (schema markup) and multimodal optimization (the visual signals AI uses to evaluate products). Most brands have started on the structured data side. Far fewer have started on the visual side, which is where the next wave of AI commerce wins are being captured.
+What's the difference between AEO and Visual AEO?
AEO focuses on making your text content readable and citable by AI answer engines, which includes schema, structured data, and listing copy. Visual AEO is the layer most brands are missing: optimizing the images that multimodal AI systems use to evaluate and recommend products. Since modern AI agents weigh both inputs, brands that only optimize text are losing recommendations to brands that optimize both.
