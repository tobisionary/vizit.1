01 — The gap

Your PIM confirms the content exists. Not that it will sell.

At new item setup, your PIM checks that every image is present, on-spec, and formatted for each retailer, then syndicates it. Nothing in that workflow checks the one thing that decides revenue: whether the images will actually make a shopper buy. Compliance is a floor, not a forecast.

FAQ

Pre-syndication scoring, answered.

+How does Vizit fit into our PIM workflow?
Your PIM calls the Vizit API when new images attach to a product record. Vizit returns Hero, Carousel, and PDP scores in seconds, inside the workflow your team already uses.
+What does the API return?
Three scores per product: Vizit Hero Score, Vizit Carousel Score, and Vizit PDP Score. Each is a 0 to 100 rating of conversion power, benchmarked against the Content Conversion Standard, with diagnostics on what's limiting conversion.
+Does Vizit replace our PIM or syndication platform?
No. Your PIM governs and syndicates content. Vizit scores whether that content will convert before it goes out. It's a quality gate on top of your stack, not a replacement.
+What happens when an asset scores below the standard?
Vizit flags it before syndication and pinpoints what's hurting conversion. Your team can fix it, or hand the spec to Vizit's Content Optimization Agent to rebuild a higher-scoring version.
+What does Vizit Certified mean?
An asset scoring 80 or above on the Content Conversion Standard. That ranks it in the top 20% of its category for conversion likelihood.
