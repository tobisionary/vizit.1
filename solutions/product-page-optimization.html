FAQ

PDP conversion questions, answered.

What ecommerce, brand, and Amazon teams ask us most about turning traffic into orders.

+How do I increase my product page conversion rate?
The highest-ROI lever on a product page is the visual content, especially the hero and carousel. These assets are the biggest drivers of conversion. Auditing every image against the Visual Conversion Standard (Vizit uses an 80+ score threshold) and fixing the ones below it is the fastest path to lift. Brands that systematically optimize visual content see an average 16.5% conversion lift.
+What is a good ecommerce conversion rate?
Ecommerce conversion rates vary by category, channel, and price point. Instead of asking "what's average for my category?", ask: "what's possible?" The truest benchmark for any PDP is the gap between your top-performing SKUs and the rest of your catalog. That gap is almost always explained by visual content losing the first-impression moment, and it's almost always closeable.
+Why isn't my Amazon listing converting?
Most underperforming Amazon listings lose at the first impression: the hero image doesn't earn the tap, so shoppers scroll past before they ever see the title, price, or reviews. The fix is almost always visual: a hero that fails on Amazon's mobile thumbnail, a carousel missing the assets shoppers expect for the category, or imagery that doesn't communicate the product's value in the half-second the shopper gives it. Vizit identifies which specific visual elements are suppressing your conversion rate and generates the fixes automatically.
+What's the difference between PDP optimization and conversion rate optimization?
Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is the broad practice of improving the percentage of visitors who take a desired action. PDP optimization is the subset focused on the product detail page itself: the surface where the actual purchase decision happens on a retail site. For ecommerce brands, PDP optimization is usually the highest-leverage CRO work, because the PDP is where most paid traffic lands and where most drop-off occurs.
+How long does it take to see results from PDP optimization?
Visual fixes deploy fast. Once an updated image is live on the PDP, the conversion impact is observed right away because every new shopper sees the optimized version immediately. Most Vizit customers see measurable lift within days of deploying Vizit Certified image updates across their priority SKUs.
+How do I optimize my Amazon listing for higher conversion?
Amazon listing optimization usually focuses on keywords, pricing, and reviews, but the largest untapped lever is visual. The hero image and image carousel determine whether shoppers tap the listing on the search results page and whether they engage once they land on the page. Vizit scores every image on the listing, identifies which ones are suppressing conversion, and generates optimized versions that are mobile-ready.
