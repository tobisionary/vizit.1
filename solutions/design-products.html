FAQ

Packaging testing questions, answered.

What innovation, insights, and packaging leaders ask us most about validating design before production.

+How do I test packaging design without surveys or focus groups?
Vizit scores digital renders of packaging concepts using our patented Perceptual AI, trained on how real shoppers respond to visual content. You can evaluate dozens of concept variations in minutes, benchmark them against category leaders, and identify the winning designs before producing a single prototype. It's the fastest packaging design testing process available — and it measures the actual purchase-driving triggers in the appearance of the pack itself.
+What is digital shelf readiness for packaging?
Digital shelf readiness is whether a packaging design performs at thumbnail scale on a mobile screen, where the majority of online purchase decisions now happen. A design that wins on a physical shelf can fail as a tiny thumbnail, because the visual cues that work at arm's length disappear at fingernail size. Vizit tests for digital shelf readiness, predicting how your packaging will perform in the moment that actually matters to omnichannel shoppers.
+How accurate is predictive packaging testing compared to in-market launches?
Vizit's predictions are validated against real commerce outcomes at 95% accuracy. The reason: focus groups and surveys measure the wrong cognitive system. Asking "Would you recommend this to a friend?" on a 1-to-5 scale activates System 2 thinking — the slow, conscious, articulated mode of the brain. But shoppers don't buy that way. Digital shelf decisions happen in System 1: fast, instinctive, visual, largely subconscious. Vizit trains on millions of real shopper interactions to measure System 1 visual response directly, which is why a concept that wins a focus group can lose on shelf — and a concept that scores high in Vizit reliably converts.
