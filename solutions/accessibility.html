04 — European Accessibility Act

Product images now fall under EU accessibility law.

Since June 2025, the European Accessibility Act has required digital commerce content sold in the EU — including product imagery and on-image text — to meet defined accessibility standards. Non-compliance carries financial penalties, market access restrictions, and growing enforcement scrutiny across member states.

What the EAA means for product imagery

  • ✓ On-image text must meet contrast and legibility thresholds aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA.
  • ✓ Critical product information communicated visually must be perceivable to shoppers with low vision and color vision deficiencies.
  • ✓ Compliance applies to brands selling into the EU, regardless of where the brand is headquartered.
  • ✓ Enforcement varies by member state, but the underlying technical standards are consistent.

FAQ

Image accessibility questions, answered.

What digital commerce, legal, and creative leaders ask us most about WCAG, APCA, and the EAA.

+What accessibility standards apply to product images?
Two standards govern text-to-background contrast in product images. WCAG 2.2 AA is the current global benchmark, required for digital accessibility compliance under ADA Title III in the U.S. and the European Accessibility Act (EAA) in Europe. APCA (Advanced Perceptual Contrast Algorithm) is the proposed contrast model under WCAG 3.0, designed to measure how human eyes actually perceive clarity on backlit digital screens.
+Does the European Accessibility Act apply to ecommerce product images?
Yes. As of June 28, 2025, the European Accessibility Act applies to B2C digital commerce in the EU, including product content where text overlays communicate critical information. The law applies to any brand selling into EU markets regardless of where the brand is headquartered, and non-compliance carries financial penalties, market access restrictions, and rising enforcement scrutiny across member states.
+How do I audit my Amazon images for readability?
Vizit automatically scans every text element in your Amazon hero and carousel images, tests contrast against both WCAG 2.2 AA and APCA standards, and provides an element-by-element scorecard. The analysis accounts for how Amazon's mobile thumbnails, search grid display, and PDP context affect actual glance-readability. Results return at catalog scale within the Vizit Platform, with the precise element-level guidance needed to fix failing assets.
+What's the penalty for non-compliant product images under accessibility law?
Penalties vary by jurisdiction. Under ADA Title III, U.S. brands face private litigation, settlement costs averaging tens of thousands of dollars per case, and ongoing remediation obligations. Under the European Accessibility Act, EU member states are implementing fines, market access restrictions, and enforcement actions that vary in severity by country. Beyond legal exposure, non-compliant imagery silently suppresses conversion: shoppers who can't read on-image text — including the 1 in 12 men with color vision deficiency — abandon products that don't communicate clearly at first glance.
