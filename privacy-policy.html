Information Collected Through Cookies and Similar Technologies

We and our service providers use cookies and other technologies to receive and store certain types of information whenever you interact with our Site through your computer or mobile device. A cookie is a small file containing a string of characters that is sent to your computer when you visit a website. When you visit the Site again, the cookie allows that site to recognize your browser. Cookies may store unique identifiers, user preferences and other information. You can reset your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some Site features or services may not function properly without cookies. We specifically use cookies to store your session ID, which is randomly generated each time you visit the Site. This session ID is used to associate specific marketing analysis requests that you make to us with your user account.

4. Our Relationship With Social Networking Sites

Our Site’s interface allows you to connect with social networking websites such as Instagram. We will work with Instagram’s application protocol interface (better known as their developer API) in a way that allows you to authorize us to read posts and public information from your Instagram account, or other accounts you specify, on your behalf. In order to provide this authorization, you will not provide us with your Instagram password, but you will need to login to Instagram directly through our VIZIT application. Once authorized by you, Instagram will provide us with a token that allows it to recognize us when we ask, on your behalf, for access to your account information. You will be able to revoke our access to Instagram at any time by amending the appropriate settings from within your account settings on Instagram’s site, though such revocation will prevent your use of portions of the Site.
